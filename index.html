The location

On Pakhowal Road, close to everything Ludhiana is known for

Hotel Kohinoor Palace sits on Pakhowal Road, one of the city's busiest corridors, so business meetings, family functions and weekend shopping are all a short drive away.

Arriving by train or bus is easy, and the airport at Sahnewal is under half an hour away for most of the day.
